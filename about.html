About the Roast

From a cart on Curry Street to a full espresso bar

Same recipe, same roaster — just more seats, and now a rider at the door.

Brewtney Café began as a single pushcart parked along Curry Street, pulling shots for early risers on their way to the pier. What started as a two-hour morning stand grew into requests for afternoon orders, then a waiting list for weekend batches.

Today the same house blend is roasted every Tuesday in small batches — sourced from growers in Kalinga and Sagada — and pulled to order across a full bar in the middle of Catbalogan City.

We kept the part people liked best: showing up wherever you already are. That's why every cup on the menu is built to travel well, packed the same day it's brewed, and handed off in cash the way the cart always did it.
