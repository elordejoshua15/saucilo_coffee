How delivery works

No app to download and no card to enter. Order from your browser, pay the rider in cash.

01

Build your order

Head to the Menu, tap "Add" on anything you want, and adjust quantities with the +/− stepper — just like adding items on a marketplace app.

02

Open your cart

The floating cart button at the bottom-right shows your running total. Tap it any time to review or edit your order.

03

Fill in delivery details

Enter your name, phone number, and address so the rider can reach you. Payment method is locked to Cash on Delivery — no online payment needed.

04

Place your order

Confirm and you'll get an order number right away. Your rider will call before arriving, usually within 30–45 minutes.

05

Pay in cash at the door

Have the total ready — subtotal plus the flat delivery fee. Exact change is appreciated but not required.
